# Optional capability menu

Choose only information that can change the current hypothesis, action, exposure, risk, or next observation. A strong trader does not expand every category on every decision.

| Capability | Use when it can distinguish | Typical material |
|---|---|---|
| Price and auction structure | trend, balance, acceptance, rejection, breakout, failed auction | multi-timeframe bars, swing structure, value/VWAP/volume profile, support/resistance |
| Microstructure and liquidity | entry quality, absorption, urgency, adverse selection | BBO, depth, spread, visible size, trades, aggressor flow, liquidity gaps |
| Derivatives positioning | leverage expansion/unwind, squeeze, carry, venue divergence | OI, funding, basis/premium, liquidation evidence, cross-venue perp state |
| Volatility and statistics | sizing, path width, regime or anomaly | realized volatility, ATR/range, distribution, momentum/mean reversion, robust comparisons |
| Cross-asset and macro | common beta, event risk, relative strength | BTC/ETH/SOL or relevant peers, rates/USD, official releases, event calendar |
| News and protocol facts | instrument-specific causal change | official announcements, governance, unlocks, protocol activity, fees/revenue |
| On-chain and participant behavior | flows or positioning not visible in venue data | public verifiable transfers, exchange flows, holder/validator activity |
| Behavioral reasoning | crowding, trapped participants, reflexivity | price/flow/positioning evidence; narrative alone is not a fact |
| Historical research | recurring or conflicting mechanisms | prior full reports, Decisions, Reviews, hypotheses and counterexamples |
| Paper/account facts | actual exposure and management obligations | admitted orders, forward fills, fees, funding, position, protection, available risk |

Use the broadest professional knowledge that is relevant, including discretionary, quantitative, microstructure, behavioral, event-driven, trend, breakout, mean-reversion, and relative-value reasoning. Do not turn this menu into a scorecard or fixed data dependency.

Missing optional data stays `UNKNOWN`. If it is essential to the decision, reduce confidence, change the action, or seek a legal public source; do not fabricate it and do not make every other capability wait.
