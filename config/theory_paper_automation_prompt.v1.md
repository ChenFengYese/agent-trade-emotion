# 72-hour new-theory paper-agent prompt v1

The sole scope and acceptance authority is
`requirements/2026-07-30-theory-paper-practice.md`. Read it before every
wake-up, keep work on that mainline, and update its progress when a milestone
actually changes.

Operate only the local credential-free paper experiment under
`.runtime/theory-paper-v1/current`. Never request, read, store, or use exchange
credentials, private APIs, signatures, or live-order capabilities.

For each hourly wake-up:

1. Verify the run manifest, transaction chain, ledger, code/theory/prompt
   bindings, and ACTIVE status.
2. If a decision is pending, do not create another cycle. Read its frozen
   market, news, analysis, portfolio context, active method delta, and decision
   template.
3. Otherwise freeze exactly one current wall-clock hourly cycle from public
   Binance USD-M data and public news discovery metadata for SNDKUSDT, MUUSDT,
   BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, and HYPEUSDT.
4. Verify material news against official primary sources when available.
   Preserve publication/retrieval time and distinguish facts, temporal
   hypotheses, behavioral inferences, and unknowns. Never identify participant
   classes as actual persons or institutions.
5. Author and submit a complete paper-only decision. Use the experimental new
   theory as the primary reasoning method. State competing hypotheses,
   support/falsifier predicates, expiry, market stage, order-flow/technical
   evidence, likely force paths, exact risk geometry, and a Chinese explanation
   for every symbol. Review every existing order and protect every open lot.
6. Do not evade action with vague uncertainty. A setup that is triggered in
   the current frozen cycle, has valid data, a falsifiable thesis,
   research-ready geometry, net reward/risk of at least 1.5 after costs, and
   passes the portfolio risk gates must be executed in that cycle. The
   six-valid-hour 100–250 USDT probe rule is only a backstop when no ordinary
   strategy trade has executed; it is not a waiting period or permission to
   postpone an already actionable setup. Abstention must identify an
   untriggered geometry, typed data-invalid state, or typed risk veto.
7. Treat manual and sealed emotional trades as exogenous disturbances. Protect
   them under the same hard risk caps and never count them as theory-originated
   success.
8. In every decision, `method_observations` must record concise, concrete notes
   for `POINT_SELECTION`, `STOP_TARGET`, `POSITION_SIZING`, `TREND_ANALYSIS`,
   `HYPOTHESIS_GENERATION`, `DIRECTION_SELECTION`, `NEWS_LINKAGE`, and
   `LOW_SCORE_OR_BLOCKER`. When an exact eight-cycle review is due, freeze it.
   Evaluate hypotheses without rewriting their original thesis; score theory
   integrity, method practice, hypothesis outcomes, and paper performance
   separately. Summarize what was actually learned in all eight method
   categories, identify every low score's concrete process, formula, data, or
   execution cause, and activate at most one future-only method delta with an
   explicit next-window test.
9. At the real 72-hour boundary, finalize only if all 72 hourly slots, six-symbol
   decisions, and nine exact reviews are present. Otherwise keep the result
   explicitly incomplete.
10. After a final report is committed, summarize the 72-hour result to this
    task and pause this heartbeat automation. Do not start a second run unless
    the user explicitly authorizes it.

Profit is an objective, not a guarantee. A 72-hour paper result is descriptive
practice evidence and cannot establish causal validity, predictive validity, or
sustainable profitability.
