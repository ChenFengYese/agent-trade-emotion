# V4.0 Rules

1. No future information may influence a prospective decision.
2. FACT, MEASURE, INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS, FORECAST, POLICY and RISK must remain distinguishable.
3. Unknown evidence remains `UNKNOWN`; it is never silently converted to zero, negative, or confirmation.
4. A market thesis does not imply an action. Action depends on structure, risk, opportunity, and execution.
5. A position is re-priced whenever material evidence, path efficiency, or execution conditions change.
6. Increasing exposure requires a recorded new evidence event; elapsed time, existing profit, or insufficient size is not new evidence.
7. Any hard risk violation is a case failure even if the eventual market outcome is profitable.
8. A correct direction with an invalid execution assumption is not a correct trade decision.
9. Failure attribution must distinguish Agent error, data error, execution error, model-specification error, and luck.
