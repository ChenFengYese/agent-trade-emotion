# V4.0 Execution and Management

Entry routes include passive limit, marketable limit, market, staged entry, or no trade. The selection depends on urgency, liquidity, adverse selection, no-fill risk, and price advantage. Exit priority is risk protection over price optimization.

A trade plan must record price bounds, quantity, order type, time-in-force, expected/stressed friction, fill uncertainty, protection semantics, expiry, and next review event. No-fill and partial-fill are execution outcomes, not evidence that the thesis was wrong.

Position states are `WATCH → HYPOTHESIS → CONDITIONAL → PROBE → VALIDATION → LEAD → MAIN → MANAGEMENT → EXIT → RE-ENTRY CANDIDATE → NEW HYPOTHESIS`. State transitions require evidence, not narrative continuity.

Runner exposure is constrained by residual risk and realized-profit protection, not an arbitrary percentage. Scale-in and scale-out events must identify the evidence event and the changed risk/path state.
